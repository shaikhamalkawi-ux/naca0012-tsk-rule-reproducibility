# Data origin, credits, and admitted checks

Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository, Airfoil Self-Noise dataset 291, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VW2C. Original authors: Thomas Brooks, D. Stuart Pope, Michael Marcolini (1989). Licensed CC BY 4.0 by UCI: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

The provided `57_H3_Airfoil_R5/data/airfoil_F5_SEMANTICALLY_VERIFIED.csv` is a researcher-prepared copy with `row_id` and `group_id` and the six experimental numeric fields. It contains 1,503 rows, 106 operating configurations and has undergone a semantic/content consistency audit against public UCI data and publicly mirrored tabular values. **We do not claim identical source-archive bytes or an official UCI ZIP SHA-256 match.** The derived row and group identifiers are non-original annotations.

The underlying benchmark concerns NACA 0012 and measured scaled sound-pressure level. Reuse is permitted by the dataset's CC BY 4.0 license with source attribution, a license reference and disclosure of changes. Do not treat this data as externally collected, newly measured, or validated for other airfoils.
